# About AcaciaFund

AcaciaFund is a research synthesis platform that distills daily insights from Hacker News and arXiv into actionable knowledge.

Our mission is to make complex information accessible and useful for decision-makers, researchers, and lifelong learners.

## Team

Our team consists of researchers, engineers, and domain experts passionate about knowledge synthesis and dissemination.
