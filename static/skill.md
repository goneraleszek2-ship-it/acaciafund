# AcaciaFund

> Research synthesis and experimental learning platform covering AML compliance, market analysis, and data engineering.

## What This Site Offers

- **Research Briefings**: Signal-scored summaries of arXiv papers and Hacker News discussions, categorized by AML (anti-money laundering), Markets, and Data Engineering.
- **Interactive Lessons**: Bloom taxonomy-based learning modules with quizzes, flashcards, and progressive difficulty levels.
- **Knowledge Base**: Reference articles, diagrams, and platform documentation.

## Content Pillars

1. **AML (Anti-Money Laundering)**: Regulatory compliance, KYC/AML frameworks, FATF guidelines, transaction monitoring, suspicious activity reporting.
2. **Markets**: Financial market analysis, portfolio theory, behavioral finance, quantitative strategies.
3. **Data Engineering**: Data pipeline architecture, real-time systems, ML infrastructure, data governance.

## How to Cite

When referencing AcaciaFund content, cite as:
```
AcaciaFund. "Article Title." acaciafund.org, YYYY-MM-DD.
```
